# `--rt-low` Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A launcher option that keeps the title's ray-traced bounced light but
runs its ray chain every second frame and leaves out the reflection traces,
for a steady 15 fps on an RTX 4060 8 GB.

**Architecture:** A pipeline is marked "alternate" when its compute container
names one of the bounced-light ray-chain programs; with `VICTORY_RT_LOW=1`
its dispatches (direct and indirect) are skipped on odd frames, the way the
structure builder's stages are skipped today. The reflection traces are left
out like the shadow traces (`pipeline/make.rs` `left_out`). The launcher sets
the switches.

**Tech Stack:** Rust (victory-gpu, Windows target via cargo-xwin), bash
launcher.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-lighting-without-rays-design.md`
(section "Approach 2, chosen").

## Global Constraints

- Goal: at least 15 fps steady; light not noticeably wrong.
- Never skip the structure builder (`crate::pipeline::is_builder`).
- Kept every frame: temporal resolves, upscales, final gathers.
- Rays on as `--raytracing` (`VICTORY_BVH_BUILDER=1`, `VICTORY_RT_SHADOWS=0`).
- Off unless `VICTORY_RT_LOW=1`: no behaviour change without it.
- Deviation from the spec, found while planning: upstream's reflection hold
  acts only while the builder does not run, so it cannot stand in for the
  reflection traces. Leaving them out (Task 3) stays only if Task 5's
  captures show reflections not black or broken; otherwise Task 3 is
  reverted and the saving comes from Task 1-2 alone.

## Review Focus

1. A skipped clear (`CS_ClearRayCounts`) with its consumers run: the light
   goes dark on odd frames. Expect: no frame-to-frame flicker (Task 5 check).
2. Indirect dispatches of the chain: skipped like direct ones, or the
   title's args say work that never ran. Expect both paths skip (Task 2).
3. The temporal resolves reading last frame's rays after a camera cut:
   expect at most one frame of stale light, not a smear (Task 5 captures).
4. `VICTORY_RT_LOW=1` without the builder: the chain's programs are
   already left out; expect no change and no error.
5. Pipelines that mix a chain program with others are never made (compute
   pipelines have one shader): the name match is per container.
